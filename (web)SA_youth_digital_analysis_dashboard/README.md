# 🌍 South Asian Youth Digital Landscape - Interactive Dashboard

An analytical research platform developed for the **SAARC Internship Program**. This dashboard provides an interactive visualization of the digital habits, risks, and access inequalities facing youth across South Asia for the 2024-2025 period.

### 🔗 Live Demo

**View the live dashboard here:** [South Asian Youth Digital Landscape - Interactive Dashboard](https://ai-mediated-digital-citizenship-data.netlify.app/)

---

## 📋 Project Overview

This project serves as a comprehensive data repository and visualization tool for social science researchers and policymakers. It focuses on the "Peak Youth" demographic (ages 18-24) and explores how device sharing, gender gaps, and platform dominance shape the digital experience in the SAARC region.

### Key Analytical Sections:

* **KPI Tracking:** Real-time overview of internet users (~2.1B) and WhatsApp penetration (83%).
* **Gender Digital Divide:** Detailed mapping of the percentage point (pp) gap between male and female users.
* **Platform Dominance:** Comparative analysis of YouTube, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram.
* **Digital Harms:** Statistical breakdown of misinformation, cyberbullying, and privacy violations.
* **The "Shared Device" Challenge:** Analysis of how 58% of South Asian youth access the web via family devices.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

* **Language:** Semantic HTML5 & CSS3 (Custom Properties/Variables for themed UI).
* **Data Visualization:** [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) (Bar charts, Line graphs, and Stacked data).
* **Responsive Design:** Mobile-first grid layouts for cross-device compatibility.
* **Analytics Logic:** Custom JavaScript for rendering six distinct data-driven visualizations.

---

## 📈 Featured Research Insights

1. **The Gender Gap:** Pakistan shows the largest divide (26.1pp), while Bhutan is the most equitable (11.0pp).
2. **Age Stratification:** Internet adoption peaks sharply in the 18-24 bracket (avg 74.25%) and falls off significantly after age 35.
3. **The Misinformation Vector:** 62% of youth across the region are affected by misinformation, primarily driven by YouTube and WhatsApp usage.
