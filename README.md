# AI-Mediated Digital Citizenship in SAARC

This repository contains research artifacts, interactive dashboards, visualization, and simulation systems developed as part of an academic internship carried out at SAARC focused on AI-mediated digital citizenship in South Asia.

> [!NOTE]
> README for each project is available in their respective folder.

## Contents
- AI-based social media feed simulators
- Rule-based and agent-based echo chamber models
- Interactive dashboards on youth digital access and harms
- Python-based analytical visualizations
- SAARC-focused AI assistant (local LLM)

## Major Projects
- Echo Chamber & Polarization Simulator
- AI-Driven Social Media Feed Simulator (LLaMA 3.2)
- SAARC AI Assistant
- South Asian Youth Digital Landscape Dashboard

## Note on Data & Simulations
All simulations are illustrative and educational. They do not claim predictive accuracy.
