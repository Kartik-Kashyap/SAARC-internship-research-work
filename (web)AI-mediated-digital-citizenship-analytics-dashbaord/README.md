# 🌐 AI-Mediated Digital Citizenship: SAARC Youth Analytics Dashboard

A high-fidelity data visualization platform focused on **South Asian digital engagement, algorithmic risks, and policy frameworks** for the years 2023–2025. This project was developed as part of a research internship focused on the SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) region.

## 📊 Project Overview

This dashboard serves as a centralized hub for cross-border analytics regarding how AI and algorithms influence digital citizenship among the youth (ages 13–25) in South Asian nations. It translates complex datasets from sources like **DataReportal, GSMA, and UNESCO** into actionable visual insights.

##### 🔗 Live Demo
View the interactive dashboard here: https://ai-mediated-digital-citizenship-data.netlify.app/

### Key Research Pillars:

* **Digital Engagement:** Tracking internet penetration and daily usage patterns.
* **Algorithmic Risks:** Analyzing the surge in AI-generated misinformation and deepfakes (APAC focus).
* **Gender Divide:** Mapping the mobile internet gap and barriers to access for women.
* **Policy Analytics:** Cataloging regional initiatives like India's *Digital Beti* and Bangladesh's *Smart Women, Smart Economy*.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Custom grid layouts and tabbed navigation).
* **Visualizations:** [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) for dynamic, interactive data rendering.
* **Data Source:** Aggregated research data (2023–2025) from official institutional reports.

---

## 📁 Features & Tabs

1. **Overview:** High-level statistics on internet penetration across the 8 SAARC nations.
2. **Digital Engagement:** Comparison of platform preferences (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) and time allocation.
3. **Algorithmic Risks:** Critical data on the **1530% surge in deepfakes** in the APAC region and their psychological impact.
4. **Gender Divide:** Visual representation of the 31% gender gap in South Asian mobile internet use.
5. **Data Tables:** Comprehensive raw data for policy researchers and academic citation.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

You only need a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari).

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/Kartik-Kashyap/SAARC-internship-research-work.git

```


2. Ensure your file structure looks like this:
```text
├── index.html
├── styles.css
├── script.js
└── analysis.png (Favicon)

```


3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

## 📈 Key Findings Highlighted

* **The 10-Hour Rule:** South Asian youth spend approximately 60% of their waking life engaged with digital platforms.
* **Algorithmic Amplification:** Misinformation spreads **6x faster** than factual content due to engagement-based algorithms.
* **Deepfake Vulnerability:** 96% of deepfakes are non-consensual, with countries like India seeing a 280% year-on-year growth.
